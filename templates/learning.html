What the predictor means

The model estimates a score from study habits, attendance, and previous marks. The bundled CSV is synthetic, generated from a formula in train_model.py; therefore its metrics are not evidence of accuracy on real students. Treat the output as a demonstration only.

Model uncertainty is not calibrated; the displayed success probability is a formula based on predicted score versus target, not a statistical confidence interval.
